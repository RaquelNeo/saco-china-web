SACO Intimates

Women’s lingerie, underwear and activewear — from design and 3D sampling to certified production for international brands.

← Back to Shanghai Saco Textile

SACO Intimates is the lingerie and underwear division of SACO Group. Since 1997 we have been developing and manufacturing durable, comfortable next-to-skin products for retailers and specialised technical brands worldwide, through strategic partner factories in China and Cambodia.

Product Categories

Bonded

Bonded technology and breathable cups for a smooth, invisible fit. Ideal for daily wear.

Seamless

Knitted on advanced seamless machines for a mark-free finish and a second-skin feel.

Cut & Sewn

Lace, microfibre and cotton styles built through cut-and-sew, with a wide range of customised designs.

Activewear

Support and comfort during movement, in breathable and durable performance materials.

Period Underwear

Leak-proof underwear that combines absorbent protection with everyday style.

Natural Dyes
